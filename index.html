NoyyinPerth (Noy in Perth)

Short, clear medical and health education videos by NoyyinPerth, a nurse based in Perth, Western Australia. Content is made in English, with Tagalog posts planned.

Videos are general health education only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 000 (Australia) or your local emergency number.

NoyyinPerth Publisher

NoyyinPerth Publisher is a private software tool that runs on my own computer. It publishes my videos to my own accounts only:

  • the NoyyinPerth channel on YouTube (YouTube API Services)
  • the NoyyinPerth Page on Facebook (Meta Graph API)
  • the NoyyinPerth account on TikTok (TikTok Content Posting API)

It isn't available to the public. There is no sign-up, and nobody else can connect an account to it.

How content is made and checked

  • English animated Shorts current: short explainers and myth-busters. The narration uses an AI-generated voice, and the animation is computer-generated.
  • Tagalog medical facts and myth photo posts planned.
  • Every medical claim is checked against trusted clinical sources, such as Healthdirect (Australian Government), the Royal Children's Hospital Melbourne and the NHS. Before anything is posted publicly, I review it personally as a clinician.
  • AI-generated voices and visuals are disclosed on each platform.
